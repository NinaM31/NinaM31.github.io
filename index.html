MOSKA-NLP at AdabEval 2026: Feature-Enriched Ensembling for Arabic Politeness Detection

Nina A. Andriyanova-Almaamary

A system for Arabic politeness classification that combines sentence embeddings with lexical, surface-level, and auxiliary signals, followed by calibrated ensembling.

Paper · Code · DOI · ·
Arabic politeness is not captured by lexical meaning alone. Formulaic expressions, style, dialect, intent, and pragmatic signals can all matter, so I wanted to test whether adding explicit signals to sentence representations could improve classification.
I designed and implemented the system, ran the experiments, and analyzed the results. The system placed 2nd in AdabEval 2026 Subtask A.
